ESA’s FLEX mission reveals the secret glow of plants

ESA – European Space Agency · Topic: Space/Infra

  • What it is: ESA's Fluorescence Explorer (FLEX) is a satellite mission designed to detect the invisible, faint glow emitted by plants during photosynthesis from space.
  • Who: The European Space Agency (ESA) is launching the FLEX mission, which will provide scientists with novel data on global plant health and activity.
  • What it does: FLEX will use its highly sensitive FLORIS instrument to map plant fluorescence globally, revealing how vegetation utilizes sunlight and responds to environmental stressors like drought.
  • Why it matters: This mission matters because it will improve understanding of vegetation's response to climate change, including drought effects, and enhance knowledge of global carbon and water cycles.
  • Strategic view: This initiative strategically highlights the increasing demand for advanced satellite-based environmental intelligence, fostering new opportunities in data analytics, climate modeling, and resilient infrastructure development for TMT.
