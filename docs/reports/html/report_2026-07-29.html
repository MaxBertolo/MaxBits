Snowflake launches Cortex AI Gateway to control AI agents and prevent runaway enterprise costs

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: Snowflake Cortex AI Gateway is a centralized control layer for governing how AI agents, both internal and external, access enterprise data, tools, and models.
  • Who: Snowflake launched this gateway, collaborating with identity vendors like 1Password, Aembit, and SailPoint, to serve enterprises deploying diverse AI agents.
  • What it does: It centrally controls AI agent access to enterprise resources, manages permissions, enforces spending limits, and provides crucial auditability and cost attribution for AI workloads.
  • Why it matters: It addresses critical enterprise security gaps and runaway costs created by autonomous AI agents, ensuring secure, auditable, and controlled AI deployment across disparate systems.

MCP just got its biggest update ever — here’s what changes for AI agents

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: The Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard connecting AI agents with software, has received its largest architectural update, making agentic AI ready for massive enterprise production deployments.
  • Who: The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation, with contributions from Anthropic, Google, AT&T, Vercel, Cloudflare, Shopify, and Amazon, led this significant protocol update.
  • What it does: The update transitions MCP to a fully stateless architecture, enabling deployment behind standard load balancers, hardening security, and graduating key features like interactive interfaces and asynchronous tasks for enhanced scalability.
  • Why it matters: This update is critical because its new stateless architecture removes the primary technical barrier, session state management, that prevented enterprise-scale deployment of AI agents in modern cloud environments.
  • Strategic view: This architectural shift democratizes enterprise AI agent adoption by enabling robust, scalable, and cloud-native deployments, paving the way for widespread automation and intelligent service delivery across TMT sectors.

Visa used Mythos to hunt for bugs in its own payment network, then open-sourced the harness that made it possible

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: Visa's "Project Glasswing" utilized Anthropic's Mythos AI to find deep vulnerabilities in its global payment network, subsequently open-sourcing a "Vulnerability Agentic Harness" for broader industry use.
  • Who: Visa, a global payment technology company, partnered with Anthropic and its Mythos AI, then provided its agentic harness to global security teams and critical infrastructure operators.
  • What it does: The harness directs AI models through structured security tasks, from threat modeling to fix validation, synthesizing exploit chains and applying candidate patches to enhance defensive capabilities.
  • Why it matters: This initiative significantly improves the ability to discover and remediate complex, chained vulnerabilities at AI speed, moving beyond traditional security scanning limitations.
  • Strategic view: This demonstrates a pivotal shift towards agentic AI for proactive cybersecurity, advocating open collaboration and new outcome-based metrics like "Mean Time to Adapt" for critical infrastructure defense.
