The three layers of agentic AI security: A defense-in-depth architecture for autonomous agents

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: This article defines a three-layered defense-in-depth security architecture to mitigate distinct risks introduced by autonomous AI agents in production environments, spanning infrastructure, network, and control plane.
  • Who: Nutanix, along with partners Intel and Cisco, advocates for a layered security approach to manage the inherent risks introduced as enterprises deploy autonomous AI agents into production.
  • What it does: The proposed architecture implements distinct security responsibilities across hardware, network, and control plane layers, preventing issues like agent hallucination, data leakage, and unauthorized access, which a single security model cannot address.
  • Why it matters: This architecture is crucial because autonomous agents, unlike traditional applications, can make decisions and act independently, necessitating specialized multi-layered security to prevent critical failures, data breaches, and ensure safe enterprise AI adoption.
  • Strategic view: C-level executives must prioritize integrating a specialized, defense-in-depth security architecture across infrastructure, network, and control planes to safely scale autonomous AI agents, mitigating new risks and ensuring robust, compliant enterprise AI deployments.
