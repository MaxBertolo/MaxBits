Prompt injection ranks No. 1 with OWASP and No. 12 in the incident record. The attack itself is invisible to a scan.

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: Prompt injection remains the top LLM application security threat according to OWASP, yet incident data shows it is underreported due to its inherent invisibility to traditional vulnerability scans.
  • Who: Security experts from OWASP and leaders in AI governance are highlighting prompt injection as a critical, yet often unseen, threat to enterprise LLM applications.
  • What it does: Prompt injection manipulates LLMs to execute attacker-desired actions by embedding instructions in legitimate data, bypassing traditional security scans and exploit detection mechanisms.
  • Why it matters: This vulnerability's invisibility to scanners means current security metrics are misleading, potentially exposing LLM-powered systems to significant, unmonitored risk from sophisticated attacks.
  • Strategic view: Executives must strategically pivot from reactive vulnerability scanning to proactive architectural defense, implementing explicit authorization gates and assuming prompt injection in all LLM system designs.

Addressing a sticking point in sustainable adhesives

MIT Technology Review – Tech · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: This news describes the development of new, environmentally friendly adhesives designed to replace pervasive petroleum-based glues, which currently hinder recycling efforts across various industries.
  • Who: Innovators and researchers are developing sustainable adhesives to benefit industries reliant on bonding materials, including construction, furniture manufacturing, and packaging, while supporting recycling infrastructure.
  • What it does: This innovation replaces existing petroleum-based glues that prevent the recycling of many products, allowing containers and other materials to be properly recycled.
  • Why it matters: It matters because petroleum-based adhesives are a widespread barrier to effective recycling, contaminating otherwise recyclable materials and contributing to waste, impacting environmental goals.
  • Strategic view: C-suite executives must consider integrating sustainable adhesives into supply chains to meet escalating ESG demands, enhance product circularity, mitigate regulatory risks, and strengthen brand reputation.
