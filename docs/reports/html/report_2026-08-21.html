VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • Who: Salesforce-owned messaging platform Slack launched "Slack Code," a new product integrating leading AI coding agents into its collaborative environment.
