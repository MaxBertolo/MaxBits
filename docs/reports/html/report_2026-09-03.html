Stolen Claude session cookies can reach corporate Gmail through grants no IT admin can revoke

VentureBeat – AI · Topic: AI/Cloud/Quantum

  • What it is: Infostealer malware replayed stolen Claude session cookies to gain unauthorized access to paid accounts, bypassing multi-factor authentication and corporate single sign-on security measures.
  • Who: Infostealer malware operators targeted individual Anthropic Claude users, specifically those with self-serve accounts, but the impact extends to corporate employees using these tools for work tasks.
  • What it does: The attack enables unauthorized access to Claude accounts, draining usage, and critically, it allows threat actors to exfiltrate sensitive data from integrated corporate services such as Gmail or Google Drive.
  • Why it matters: This incident reveals a significant security vulnerability where personal use of AI tools on corporate devices bypasses traditional enterprise IT security controls, exposing sensitive organizational data.
  • Strategic view: C-level executives must prioritize implementing comprehensive policies for shadow AI usage and securing third-party integrations, alongside robust employee education, to mitigate critical enterprise data exfiltration risks.
